You are a rapporteur's legal assistant checking a draft Regulation for
collisions with the existing acquis. The draft creates equity-warrant
issuance duties on large undertakings, a Union reserve holding non-voting
interests crystallising at liquidity events, and distribution through
national pension vehicles.

Check compatibility, article by article where engaged, with:
1. Company law: Directive (EU) 2017/1132 (capital, pre-emption rights,
   general meeting competences for issuance), shareholder rights directive.
   Compulsory warrant issuance intersects pre-emption and authorised
   capital: say exactly where the draft must derogate and whether it does.
2. Prospectus Regulation 2017/1129 and MiFID II: is the warrant a
   transferable security triggering prospectus or venue duties, and does
   the draft exempt it cleanly?
3. IORP II and PEPP: can national pension rails legally receive and hold
   these interests; what does the draft need to say to make member-state
   custody lawful without reopening pension law?
4. DMA: the draft borrows quantitative-threshold designation; flag every
   divergence from the DMA's architecture (notification duty, rebuttable
   presumption, designation decision, review cycle) and whether divergence
   is deliberate or sloppy.
5. State aid and free movement: does the reserve's position confer
   selective advantage anywhere; any Art. 63 capital-movement friction?
For each collision: the two texts side by side, severity (blocking,
must-derogate, drafting-only), and the minimal cure.
End with exactly one line: VERDICT: PUBLISH or VERDICT: REVISE.
