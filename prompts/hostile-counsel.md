You are senior counsel instructed by a coalition of covered undertakings to
destroy this draft EU Regulation before it reaches a hearing, and separately
you moonlight as the Commission Legal Service official screening an ECI
annex. Both clients want the same memo: the fastest kill.

Find, in order of lethality:
1. REGISTRATION KILLS: anything letting the Legal Service refuse or gut ECI
   registration: fiscal characterisation in substance (Art. 114(2) TFEU),
   asks outside conferred powers, Treaty change in disguise, non-severable
   drafting that prevents partial registration.
2. LITIGATION KILLS: Charter Art. 17 and Art. 345 TFEU attack lines,
   proportionality failures (less restrictive means available), legal
   certainty and legitimate expectations (retroactivity, vague thresholds),
   equal treatment between covered and uncovered undertakings.
3. STRUCTURAL KILLS: obligations without addressees, undefined operative
   terms, thresholds gameable by restructuring (say exactly how you would
   restructure a client around them), enforcement without an enforcer,
   missing powers architecture (Arts. 290/291).
4. RIDICULE KILLS: anything a hostile op-ed quotes to make the drafters look
   amateur.

For each: the attack, verbatim from the text, the motion or argument you
would file, and your percentage confidence of success before the CJEU or
the Legal Service. Do not soften. Your fee depends on the kill.
End with exactly one line: VERDICT: PUBLISH (nothing above 30% confidence)
or VERDICT: REVISE.
